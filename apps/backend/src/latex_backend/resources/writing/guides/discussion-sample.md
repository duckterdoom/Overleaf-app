## 2. Model answer (teaching example)

While one school of thought contends that apartment complexes represent the most advantageous form of modern housing, another segment of the population maintains that detached private houses provide a superior domestic lifestyle. Both perspectives possess legitimate justifications, and this essay will evaluate both arguments before presenting a balanced verdict grounded in household financial capabilities.

On the one hand, advocating for apartments is primarily driven by arguments regarding land-use efficiency and economic affordability. Constructing multi-story residential towers upon a single parcel of land yields an extensive number of housing units, which significantly lowers acquisition and construction expenses per household relative to standalone properties. Additionally, modern apartment complexes incorporate shared amenities, including communal fitness spaces, 24-hour security, and recreational facilities, which minimize travel requirements and lower ancillary living expenditures. Because working families with modest or median incomes comprise the vast majority of the urban demographic, apartments offer an indispensable, cost-effective residential solution that makes urban property ownership attainable.

On the other hand, the preference for detached private houses is firmly grounded in requirements for domestic privacy and long-term financial appreciation. Living in close proximity to numerous neighbors within an apartment building inherently risks acoustic intrusions and communal friction, which can disrupt personal well-being. Detached dwellings, by contrast, possess defined physical perimeters that insulate occupants from external disturbances while granting families complete autonomy over their living environment. Furthermore, detached houses entail direct ownership of the underlying land, an asset class that consistently demonstrates robust capital appreciation over time, unlike high-rise apartments which often face structural depreciation and escalating maintenance levies.

In conclusion, while apartments deliver essential affordability and communal convenience for the broader public, detached houses provide unparalleled privacy and serve as potent wealth-generating assets. In my opinion, neither housing model can be deemed universally superior; rather, the optimal choice must be decided on an individual basis according to a family's financial resources and life stage.

## 3. Sentence-by-Sentence Breakdown & Analysis
- **Introduction**: Employs the *schools of thought* framework to reframe the dichotomy with lexical maturity (*advocating for apartments*, *detached private houses*).
- **Body 1 (Apartment Merits)**: Anchors on spatial efficiency; explains why land sharing lowers per-unit development expenditure; details how bundled amenities reduce secondary spending; grounds in urban demographic realities.
- **Body 2 (Private House Merits)**: Highlights acoustic intrusion and communal friction; demonstrates how a physical perimeter guarantees insulation; contrasts appreciating land equity against depreciating structures.
- **Conclusion**: Resolves the tension through a lifecycle and resource framework without dogmatic bias.
