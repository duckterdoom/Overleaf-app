## 2. Model answer (teaching example)

The diagram illustrates the sequential stages involved in the commercial production of leather goods from raw animal hides.

Overall, the procedure is a linear, multi-stage industrial process that commences with the initial drying of raw skins and culminates in the manufacture of various leather consumer items. In addition, the material is subjected to two distinct transportation phases alongside a series of chemical immersions, mechanical flattening, and finishing treatments.

At the beginning of the process, raw animal skins are hung outdoors to dry thoroughly before being loaded onto a truck and transported to a processing facility. Upon arrival, the skins are first washed in a tank containing a mixture of water and lime to cleanse them. Following this washing stage, the hides are immersed in a concentrated lime solution in a large vat, which softens the material and prepares it for further treatment.

Once conditioned, the skins are fed through mechanical rollers to be flattened to a uniform thickness. The flattened material then undergoes a crucial tanning phase, during which it is submerged in a specialized liquid composed of water and tannins derived from vegetable matter. After tanning, the resulting leather is mechanically polished to attain a refined finish. Finally, the leather is transported to manufacturing plants, where it is turned into everyday consumer products, including footwear, handbags, and sports equipment.

- **Introduction**: Nominalizes the prompt cleanly (*the sequential stages involved in the commercial production of leather goods from raw animal hides*).
- **Overview**: Outlines the linear beginning and culmination, while highlighting structural features like the two transportation steps and chemical baths.
- **Body 1 (Initial Phases)**: Connects drying, first transport, and water-lime washing with disciplined present passive constructions (*are hung outdoors*, *being loaded*, *are first washed*, *are immersed*).
- **Body 2 (Mechanical & Chemical Finishing)**: Connects flattening, vegetable tannin bath, mechanical polishing, and consumer output fabrication (*is fed through*, *is submerged*, *mechanically polished*, *transported to manufacturing plants*).
