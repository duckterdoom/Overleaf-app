## 2. Model answer

The pie charts compare the labelled shares of five categories of books sold by a bookseller in 1972, 1992 and 2012.

Overall, adult fiction increased its share and became the largest category by the final year. Biography and other books lost share, while the labels for travel and children's fiction changed less consistently.

In 1972, adult fiction, children's fiction and biography each accounted for 20%. The label for other books was 29%, making it the largest single category, while travel stood at 10%. In 1992, adult fiction increased to 25% and children's fiction to 22%, whereas biography fell to 13% and other books to 20%. Travel was again labelled 10%.

By 2012, adult fiction had risen to 40%. Children's fiction was labelled 20%, matching its initial figure. Biography declined further to 8%, while travel and other books were each shown at 12%. These figures describe the labels supplied in the visual; the source totals require verification.

## 3. How this sample is organised

- **Introduction:** Identify the subject, time frame and units.
- **Overview:** State the main patterns without listing every figure.
- **Body:** Group related details and compare them using the supplied visual.
