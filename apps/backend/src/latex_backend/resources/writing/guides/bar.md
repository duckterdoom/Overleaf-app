## 2. Segment & Sentence-by-Sentence Blueprint

### Introduction (1 Sentence)
- **Sentence 1**: *The chart illustrates the tonnage of four distinct waste materials—paper, glass, tins, and garden waste—collected by a recycling center over a four-year period from 2011 to 2015, measured in metric tons.*

### Overview (2 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Dominance & Hierarchy)**: *Overall, paper and glass consistently accounted for the vast majority of recycled waste, with both finishing well above the remaining categories.*
- **Sentence 2 (Distinct Movement & Volatility)**: *In addition, while paper rebounded to finish at a clear peak after an early decline, garden waste declined sharply in 2012 before recovering.*

### Body 1: Major Categories (3–4 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Primary Category Baseline & Dip)**: *Paper collections initially stood at 57 tons in 2011 before dropping progressively to 50 in 2012 and hitting a low of 40 tons in 2013.*
- **Sentence 2 (Rebound & Culmination)**: *Following this contraction, it recovered sharply over the next two years to conclude at a distinct peak of 70 tons in 2015.*
- **Sentence 3 (Secondary Leader & Crossover Point)**: *By contrast, glass fluctuated within a narrower band; starting at 48 tons, it dipped to 41 in 2012 before climbing to 48 tons in 2013, briefly becoming the only material to overtake paper before reaching 46 in 2014 and 52 tons in 2015.*

### Body 2: Minor Categories (3–4 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Lower Bracket Introduction & Tins Path)**: *The remaining materials were collected in markedly smaller quantities. Tins began at 35 tons, dropped to 27 in 2012, recovered to 34 the following year, and closed at 39 tons after dipping marginally in 2014.*
- **Sentence 2 (Erratic / Volatile Companion)**: *Garden waste displayed a substantially more volatile trajectory, falling from 32 tons in 2011 to 15 in 2012, before recovering to 31 in 2013, slipping to 27 in 2014 and reaching 34 tons by the end of the period.*

---

## 3. Recommended Vocabulary, Collocations & Phrasal Frames
- **Describing Volume Brackets**: *collected in markedly smaller quantities*, *accounted for the vast majority of...*
- **Trajectories & Fluctuations**: *narrower band of fluctuation*, *recovered sharply*, *oscillating between [X] and [Y]*, *displayed a substantially more volatile trajectory*.
- **Peak and Standing Phrases**: *finish at a clear peak*, *briefly becoming the only material to overtake...*, *tapered slightly to...*

---

## 4. Critical Traps & Band-Draining Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Repeating Measurement Units Everywhere**: State the unit once at the start of the clause (*57 tons in 2011 before dropping progressively to 50 in 2012 and 40 in 2013*).
2. **Missing the Crossover Event**: If two dominant categories cross paths (e.g., Glass overtaking Paper in 2013), failing to mention it robs your essay of key Task Achievement points.
