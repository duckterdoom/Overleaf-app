## 2. Segment & Sentence-by-Sentence Blueprint

### Introduction (1 Sentence)
- **Sentence 1**: *The three pie charts illustrate the proportion of book sales accounted for by five different genres—adult fiction, children's fiction, biography, travel, and others—at an independent bookstore in 1972, 1992, and 2012.*

### Overview (2 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Dominance Surge)**: *Overall, the sales distribution became increasingly skewed over the 40-year period, with adult fiction surging to become the dominant category by a wide margin.*
- **Sentence 2 (Contraction of Minor Genres)**: *In contrast, biography and miscellaneous titles commanded smaller labelled market shares at the end of the timeframe compared to their starting positions.*

### Body 1: The Balanced Decades (1972 & 1992) (3–4 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Initial Distribution Equivalence)**: *In 1972, book sales were distributed relatively evenly across most genres, with adult fiction, children's fiction, and biography each capturing exactly 20% of the market.*
- **Sentence 2 (Initial Minor Categories)**: *The remaining market was divided between other genres, which held the largest individual slice at 29%, and travel books at 10%.*
- **Sentence 3 (The 1992 Transition)**: *By 1992, the pattern began to shift: adult fiction and children's fiction expanded to 25% and 22% respectively, while travel remained at 10%, biography fell to 13%, and other books to 20%.*

### Body 2: Polarization & Market Dominance (2012) (3–4 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Market Leader Explosion)**: *By 2012, this trend accelerated dramatically as adult fiction surged to account for 40% of labelled sales.*
- **Sentence 2 (Steady Secondary Performer)**: *Children's fiction also solidified its position, returning to its initial 20% share.*
- **Sentence 3 (Dramatic Squeeze on Other Genres)**: *Conversely, biography fell to 8%, while travel was labelled 12%, while miscellaneous books accounted for only 12%.*

---

## 3. Recommended Vocabulary, Collocations & Phrasal Frames
- **Market Share Verbs**: *captured 20% of the market*, *accounted for nearly half of total sales*, *constituted a quarter of overall turnover*.
- **Structural Shifts**: *sales distribution became increasingly skewed*, *distributed relatively evenly across most genres*, *suffered severe contractions*, *lost ground*.
- **Prepositional & Margin Phrases**: *by a wide margin*, *in stark contrast to their starting positions*.

---

## 4. Critical Traps & Band-Draining Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Mechanical Slicing**: Describing every slice of 1972, then every slice of 1992, then every slice of 2012 creates extreme repetition. Connect categories across time periods.
2. **Confusing Percentage with Real Volume**: Do not claim "sales increased by 25 books." It is *percentage share of total sales*, not absolute count.
