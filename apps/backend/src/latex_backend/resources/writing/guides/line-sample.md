## 2. Model answer

The line graph compares the percentage of people satisfied with the healthcare system in Canada, the UK, the Netherlands and the US in 1991, 1999 and 2007.

Overall, satisfaction in Canada fell sharply and then partially recovered, while the Netherlands had the highest figure in the final two years. The US remained the lowest throughout, and both the UK and the US recorded modest increases.

Canada began at approximately 59% in 1991, ahead of the Netherlands at about 48%. By 1999, the Canadian figure had dropped to around 18%, whereas the Dutch figure edged up to approximately 49%. Canada subsequently recovered to about 39% in 2007, but remained below the Netherlands, whose figure declined slightly to roughly 46%.

The UK showed a steadier upward pattern, rising from approximately 23% in 1991 to 28% in 1999 and nearly 30% in 2007. The US experienced a much smaller increase, from around 10% to 11% and then 12%.
