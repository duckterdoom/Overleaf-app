## 2. Segment & Sentence-by-Sentence Blueprint

### Introduction (1 Sentence)
- **Sentence 1**: *The diagram illustrates the sequential stages involved in the commercial production of leather products, starting from raw animal skins through to finished consumer items.*

### Overview (2 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Boundary Milestones)**: *Overall, the process is a linear, multi-stage industrial procedure that begins with the drying of animal skins and culminates in the manufacturing of various leather goods.*
- **Sentence 2 (Operational Complexity & Logistics)**: *It is also noticeable that the skins undergo two distinct transportation phases alongside a combination of chemical washings, mechanical flattening, and polishing.*

### Body 1: Phase 1 — Extraction, Transport & Chemical Washing (3 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Drying & Initial Logistics)**: *In the initial stage, raw animal skins are hung out in the open air to dry thoroughly before being loaded onto a truck and transported to a specialized processing factory.*
- **Sentence 2 (Initial Lime Wash)**: *Upon arrival at the facility, the hides are submerged and washed in a large container filled with a mixture of water and lime.*
- **Sentence 3 (Lime Soaking)**: *Following this cleansing process, the material is transferred to a dedicated vat where it is soaked in lime solution to facilitate softening and remove impurities.*

### Body 2: Phase 2 — Flattening, Tanning & Product Fabrication (3–4 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Flattening Rollers)**: *Once sufficiently conditioned, the skins are passed through mechanical rollers to be flattened to a uniform thickness.*
- **Sentence 2 (Tannin Bath / Chemical Fixation)**: *Subsequently, the flattened hides undergo another immersion phase, this time in a tanning solution composed of water and tannins derived from vegetable sources.*
- **Sentence 3 (Polishing)**: *After the tanning phase is complete, the leather is polished to achieve a smooth texture.*
- **Sentence 4 (Final Manufacturing & Transport)**: *Finally, the prepared leather is transported to manufacturers, where it is crafted into commercial consumer products such as shoes, bags, and sporting goods.*

## 3. Recommended Vocabulary, Collocations & Phrasal Frames
- **Sequential Linkers**: *In the initial stage...*, *Upon arrival at the facility...*, *Following this cleansing process...*, *Once sufficiently conditioned...*, *Subsequently...*, *Finally...*
- **Passive Verb Collocations**: *are hung out in the open air*, *are submerged and washed*, *passed through mechanical rollers*, *flattened to a uniform thickness*, *undergo another immersion phase*, *polished to achieve a smooth texture*.
- **Process Classifiers**: *linear, multi-stage industrial procedure*, *undergo two distinct transportation phases*, *culminates in the manufacturing of...*

---

## 4. Critical Traps & Band-Draining Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Using Past Tense**: Writing "the skins were washed and dried" is a fatal error. General recurring processes require **Present Simple Passive** (*are washed, are dried*).
2. **Missing Intermediary Details**: Skipping transportation steps or chemical bath ingredients reduces Task Achievement.
