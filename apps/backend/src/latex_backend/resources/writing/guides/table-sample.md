## 2. Model answer

The tables compare student exchanges between five European countries and Australia in 2007 and 2009.

Overall, more students travelled from Europe to Australia than in the opposite direction. The reported European total increased, whereas the Australian total remained unchanged. Germany showed the largest rise among European sending countries, and France was the most popular destination for Australian students in both years.

The reported total travelling to Australia rose from 186 to 226. France supplied the largest number in 2007, at 61, followed by the Netherlands at 36. In 2009, Germany became the largest contributor, increasing from 27 to 63, while Sweden rose from 32 to 42. France edged down to 60, the UK fell from 31 to 25, and the Netherlands remained at 36.

The Australian total was 152 in both years. France attracted 37 students in 2007 and 58 in 2009, while Germany increased from 18 to 26. In contrast, Sweden declined from 33 to 27, the UK from 32 to 23, and the Netherlands from 32 to 18.
