## 2. Model answer

The bar chart compares the amounts of paper, glass, tins and garden waste collected by a recycling centre from 2011 to 2015, in tons.

Overall, paper and glass accounted for the largest collections. Paper fluctuated considerably before reaching the highest figure in the final year, while tins and garden waste remained lower and changed less dramatically.

Paper collections fell from 57 tons in 2011 to 50 in 2012 and 40 in 2013, before recovering to 51 in 2014 and 70 in 2015. Glass started at 48 tons, dropped to 41 in 2012, and returned to 48 in 2013, when it exceeded paper. It then slipped to 46 tons before finishing at 52.

Tins declined from 35 tons to 27 between 2011 and 2012, recovered to 34 in 2013, and stood at 33 in 2014 before reaching 39 in 2015. Garden waste followed a relatively narrow range: 32, 15, 31, 27 and 34 tons respectively. Its lowest collection was therefore in 2012.
