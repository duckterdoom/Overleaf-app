## 2. Model answer

The diagrams show the current layout of a university biomedical campus and its proposed redevelopment.

Overall, the plan adds specialised research facilities to the open area in the upper part of the campus and changes circulation and parking. Several existing laboratories remain, while the central road is removed and the Biotech building is enlarged.

Currently, the upper area contains trees and a car park on the right. The plan introduces Genetics 1 on the upper left and Bioimaging nearer the centre, while retaining some trees. The existing car park is designated for students.

In the lower area, the left-hand laboratory and the laboratory on the right remain. The Genetics building becomes Genetics 2, and the Biotech facility below it expands considerably. Biochemistry is retained but shown in a different orientation. The central walkway remains, while the horizontal road through the site is removed. A diagonal road in the lower-left area gives access to a designated staff car park.

## 3. How this sample is organised

- **Introduction:** Identify the subject, time frame and units.
- **Overview:** State the main patterns without listing every figure.
- **Body:** Group related details and compare them using the supplied visual.
