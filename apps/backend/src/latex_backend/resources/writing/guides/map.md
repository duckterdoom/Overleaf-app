# IELTS Writing Task 1: Map — Master Guide & Template (v2)

## 1. Visual Prompt & Topic Framing

![University Campus Redevelopment Map](/api/writing/guide-assets/080b9c26e4541234264d5dbc7de5228332925dd038e1ab015553395332982660.jpg)
*Figure 1: Current layout of a university campus vs. proposed redevelopment plan*

- **Visual Capture**: Spatial layouts depicting a site at present vs. proposed future redevelopment.
- **Core Mental Model**: Avoid random wandering. Divide the campus by geographic zones: **Northern Sector** in Body 1, **Southern & Road Infrastructure** in Body 2. Use prospective future forms (*is projected to*, *will be constructed*, *is slated for expansion*).

---

## 2. Segment & Sentence-by-Sentence Blueprint

### Introduction (1 Sentence)
- **Sentence 1**: *The maps illustrate the current layout of a university campus alongside proposed redevelopment plans for its future expansion.*

### Overview (2 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Structural Densification & Purpose)**: *Overall, the campus will transition into a significantly more modernized and densely built academic environment, characterized by the expansion of research facilities at the expense of open green space.*
- **Sentence 2 (Zoning & Segregated Infrastructure)**: *Furthermore, the transport network will be reconfigured with vehicular roads shifted away from the center, accompanied by the introduction of segregated parking facilities for students and university staff.*

### Body 1: Northern Sector Developments (3 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Present Baseline)**: *At present, the northern portion of the site is dominated by open lawn areas and trees, bounded by a generic car park in the northeast.*
- **Sentence 2 (Academic Construction)**: *Under the proposed plan, while some vegetation will be preserved, this area will accommodate two new academic facilities: a Genetics building in the northwest corner and a Bio-imaging center positioned in the center-north.*
- **Sentence 3 (Parking Redesignation)**: *Additionally, the existing car park in the east is slated to be converted into a dedicated student parking facility.*

### Body 2: Southern Sector & Transport Reconfiguration (4 Sentences)
- **Sentence 1 (Present Lab Configuration & Walkway)**: *In the southern section, the current layout comprises three adjacent facilities—Biochemistry, Genetics, and Biotech—bisected by a central pedestrian walkway.*
- **Sentence 2 (Building Reconfiguration & Expansions)**: *In the future scheme, the pedestrian path will be retained, but the Biochemistry unit will be retained in a different orientation; meanwhile, Genetics will be expanded and renamed Genetics 2, and the Biotech complex will be enlarged substantially.*
- **Sentence 3 (Road Network Rerouting)**: *Crucially, the road currently running through the campus center will be abolished and reconfigured with a diagonal access link in the lower-left area.*
- **Sentence 4 (Dedicated Staff Car Park)**: *Finally, a newly constructed staff car park will be added in the southwestern corner to serve university personnel.*

## 3. Recommended Vocabulary, Collocations & Phrasal Frames
- **Directional & Spatial Collocations**: *in the northern portion of the site*, *bounded by...*, *in the northwest corner*, *positioned in the center-north*, *looping around the southern perimeter*, *in the southwestern corner*.
- **Development & Modification Verbs**: *will transition into a significantly more modernized environment*, *accommodate two new academic facilities*, *is slated to be converted into...*, *bisected by a central pedestrian walkway*, *enlarged substantially*, *abolished and replaced by...*
- **Zoning & Purpose Vocabulary**: *segregated parking facilities*, *at the expense of open green space*, *dedicated student parking facility*.

---

## 4. Critical Traps & Band-Draining Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Radar / Ping-Pong Description**: Bouncing from south to north to center disorients the examiner. Anchor your writing geographically (North vs. South).
2. **Confusing Tenses**: Using past tense ("the road was built") is inaccurate for proposed plans. Use future passive (*will be constructed, is slated to be replaced, will be introduced*).
